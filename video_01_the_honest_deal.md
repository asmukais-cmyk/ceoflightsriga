# Video 01 — "The Honest Deal"
## Austin | Riga Office | First Recruitment Video

**Type:** Instagram Reel / TikTok / Facebook  
**Length:** 60–90 seconds  
**Filmed by:** Someone holding Austin's phone (Casper or Carolina), or phone on a tripod  
**Gear needed:** Phone only. No lights, no mic unless you have a lav clip.

---

## The Story Arc

| Beat | What's happening | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | Austin walking through the office, camera follows | 5 sec |
| **Who I am** | Austin stops, talks to camera | 10 sec |
| **Show the desks** | Cut to the empty workstations | 5 sec |
| **The honest pitch** | Austin talking — what we want, what it takes | 30 sec |
| **The offer** | What you get — training, desk, supervisor | 10 sec |
| **The filter** | Who this ISN'T for | 10 sec |
| **CTA** | Apply / DM / link | 5 sec |

Total: ~75 seconds

---

## Shot-by-Shot Breakdown

### SHOT 1 — The Walk-In (5 sec)
**What to film:** Austin walking into the main office area. Camera follows from behind or beside him, then he turns to face camera.  
**Location:** Start from the hallway/entrance, walk toward the desk area.  
**Audio:** Austin starts talking mid-walk.  
**Line:**
> *"I have 12 empty desks in my office right now."*

**Notes:** Keep moving. Don't stop walking until the line is done. The movement makes the hook dynamic.

---

### SHOT 2 — The Introduction (10 sec)
**What to film:** Austin standing, facing camera. Upper body. Office visible behind him.  
**Location:** Standing near the glass meeting room wall (world clocks visible behind = great visual).  
**Line:**
> *"My name's Austin. I started a company called CEOFLIGHTS. We sell international business class flights to American clients — and we do it from right here in Riga."*

**Notes:** Conversational. Like you're telling a friend, not presenting.

---

### SHOT 3 — The Empty Desks (5 sec)
**What to film:** Slow pan across the empty workstations — the blue dividers, monitors, headsets. No people. Just the setup waiting.  
**Location:** The main desk area (Office3.jpeg angle is perfect).  
**Audio:** Austin voiceover continues, OR text overlay: **"12 spots. All equipped."**  
**Line (voiceover, optional):**
> *"Let me tell you exactly who I want sitting in them."*

**Notes:** This is the visual money shot. The emptiness tells the story. Slow and steady pan — no shaky rush.

---

### SHOT 4 — The Honest Pitch (30 sec)
**What to film:** Austin talking to camera. This is the core. One continuous take.  
**Location:** Same spot as Shot 2, or sitting on the red lounge sofas for a different feel.  
**Line (loose — know the beats, don't memorize):**
> *"I'm not looking for a perfect resume. I don't care if you've never sold anything in your life. What I care about is: can you take a hard day and come back the next morning ready to go again?*
>
> *Because sales is hard. There are days where nothing works. Where you feel like you're going backwards. The people who make it here are the ones who don't quit on those days. They push through, they figure it out, they build their own system.*
>
> *And when they do — they earn well. Commission-based. No ceiling. Your effort is your paycheck."*

**Notes:** 
- This is the section where you slow down and be real. No rush.  
- The line *"can you take a hard day and come back the next morning"* — pause slightly after it. Let it land.
- If you mess up a line, just keep going. The imperfection is the point.

---

### SHOT 5 — The Offer (10 sec)
**What to film:** Austin gesturing around the office, or walking past a desk and tapping it.  
**Location:** Standing near a workstation.  
**Line:**
> *"You'll work out of this office. Everything's here — desk, computer, headset. We train you from scratch. Our supervisor Casper is here with you every day. You're not doing this alone."*

**Notes:** Physical gestures help here — point at the desk, touch the chair. Makes it tangible.

---

### SHOT 6 — The Filter (10 sec)
**What to film:** Austin, direct to camera. Slightly closer framing.  
**Location:** Same as Shot 4 or 5.  
**Line:**
> *"If you want a comfortable 9-to-5 where you clock in and clock out — this isn't it. If you want to develop yourself, work hard, and get paid for your effort — we're looking for you."*

**Notes:** This should have a slight edge. Not aggressive, but clear. You're telling people it's okay to not apply. That's powerful.

---

### SHOT 7 — CTA (5 sec)
**What to film:** Austin, slight smile, direct.  
**Location:** Same.  
**Line:**
> *"Link in bio. Come talk to us."*

**Notes:** Short. Done. Don't oversell the ending.

---

## How to Shoot This in Practice

You don't need to film 7 separate clips perfectly. Here's the simplest approach:

### Option A — "Walk and talk" (easiest)
1. Someone holds the phone and follows Austin as he walks through the office
2. Austin delivers the whole thing in one take while moving through the space
3. No cuts needed — the movement IS the editing
4. Walk in → stop at the desks → deliver the pitch → end at a workstation → CTA

### Option B — "3 setups" (slightly more polished)
1. **Setup 1:** Walking shot for the hook (Shot 1)
2. **Setup 2:** Static — Austin talking at the world clocks wall (Shots 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 — all in one go)
3. **Setup 3:** B-roll pan of empty desks (Shot 3) — can film this separately, even after

Then someone cuts it together on CapCut or InShot in 20 minutes.

## Pre-Shoot Checklist

- [ ] Phone charged, storage clear
- [ ] Office lights on (the overhead strips look good — use them)
- [ ] Desks clean and empty (no random items on them)
- [ ] World clocks visible in at least one shot
- [ ] Someone to hold the phone OR tripod set up
- [ ] Austin knows the 7 beats (not the exact words — just the beats)
- [ ] Film horizontal for YouTube/Facebook, vertical for TikTok/Reels — or just film vertical and crop later
