# CEOFLIGHTS Riga Office — Social Media Recruitment Video Strategy

Last updated: 2026-05-01

## Objective
Generate a robust pipeline of interested candidates for the Riga, Latvia office by the end of May 2026. The goal is to fill **12 available workstations** with hardworking, ambitious people who want to earn real money — no prior experience required.

## Target Audience
- **Age:** 18 to 29 years old
- **Location:** Riga, Latvia
- **Profile:** Ambitious and motivated, but a regular person — not someone chasing a corporate ladder. They want good income, a real team, and a skill that pays. No airline or sales experience needed.
- **Role:** Sales agents selling international business class flights to U.S. customers. Commission-based. Real earning upside for people willing to work.

## Positioning — What We Are (and Are NOT)

### ✅ What we ARE
- A **lean, no-BS company** where regular people come in, learn a skill, and earn good money
- A **real office** in central Riga — desks, computers, headsets, everything provided — you just show up
- A place where **effort directly equals income** — the more you close, the more you make
- **Small team, flat structure** — no giant corporation, no endless bureaucracy
- Run by people who are **normal and approachable** — Casper is right there on the floor with you every day
- A place where **you learn something valuable**: how to sell, how to handle U.S. clients, how international travel works

### ❌ What we are NOT
- Not elite, not posh, not "luxury lifestyle" flexing
- Not a corporate job where you sit in meetings all day doing nothing
- Not a scam or MLM — it's a straightforward sales job with transparent commissions
- Not for people who want to coast — it's for people who want to earn

## Tone & Style
- **Genuine, real, unscripted** — phone-camera energy is fine, even preferred for organic formats
- **Casper talks like a normal person** — no polished presenter voice, just straight and honest
- Humor and self-awareness are welcome — relatable > polished
- Show the actual office as-is: it's clean, modern, functional — not a showroom, not a basement
- The message is: *"This is a real place, with real people, doing a real job that pays well."*

## Key Personnel & Office Context
- **Austin:** Founder of CEOFLIGHTS (U.S.-based). Appears in video content as the person who built the company and set the vision.
- **Casper:** On-site supervisor in Riga — trainer, manager, face of the office content. The person candidates will actually work with every day.
- **Carolina:** HR. Handles applications, interviews, and candidate onboarding. The first human touchpoint for applicants.
- **The office:** Central Riga. Fully equipped — 12 desks with computers, headsets, acoustic dividers, a meeting room, a lounge area. Everything is ready. Seats are empty. That's the story.

## Content Formats & Ideas

### 🎥 Format 1 — "The Honest Job Post" (Direct address)
Casper to camera. No script, just honest talk.
> *"We have 12 desks sitting empty in our Riga office. We sell business class flights to American clients. It's commission-based. If you're willing to learn and willing to work — you can make good money here. DM us or apply in the link."*

### 🎥 Format 2 — Office Tour (Walkthrough, no hype)
Simple phone walkthrough of the space. Show the desks, the headsets, the lounge, the clocks on the wall. Caption: *"12 spots. All equipped. Waiting to be filled."*

### 🎥 Format 3 — "Day in the Life" (Authentic)
Casper or a team member showing what a real day looks like. Morning, first calls, lunch break, afternoon push. Normal. Not glamorized.

### 🎥 Format 4 — Myth-busting / Relatable Skits
Short, punchy. Things like:
- *"Things people think working here means vs. what it actually means"*
- *"What I thought sales was vs. what it actually is"*
- *"You don't need to know anything about flights to start"*

### 🎥 Format 5 — The Earnings Reality Check
Transparent and grounded. Not "make 10k your first week." More like:
> *"Our agents are commission-based. Some months are slow when you're learning. Once you get the hang of it — the upside is real. We're not hiding that."*

### 🎥 Format 6 — "Why Riga?" angle
For the local audience — acknowledge you're a Latvian-based team working with a U.S. company. That's actually interesting and unusual. Lean into it.

## Platforms & Distribution
- **TikTok** — highest reach for 18–25 Riga audience. Organic first.
- **Instagram Reels + Stories** — repurpose TikTok content. Stories for direct CTA.
- **Facebook Videos** — for slightly older candidates (25–29), and for reach via local groups/sharing.

## Strategic Goal
Find **one or two content formats** that Casper and the team genuinely enjoy making — then repeat that format consistently at volume. Consistency > perfection. The office photos and real space are strong assets. Use them as-is.

## What to Avoid
- Overly produced or scripted content
- "Luxury lifestyle" or money-flex framing — this is not that company
- Vague job descriptions — be specific about what the role is
- Overselling — the audience will trust honest more than hype

## Who We Are Looking For — Agent Requirements

*These are the core criteria for every agent we hire. They should inform all content — what we say, how we say it, and who we attract. Being explicit about these in videos naturally filters for the right people.*

### 1. Full Commitment — Life Must Allow It
This is not a 9-to-5 you can switch off at the end of your shift. In the early months, you have to eat and breathe sales. That means **extra hours, weekend availability, being reachable when the customer needs you** — not just when it's convenient. Realistically, expect 10–12 hour days while you're getting up to speed.

This has a real personal cost: your relationship with your girlfriend or boyfriend may suffer. Your social life takes a back seat. Work-life balance is not something you can expect in the first few months.

**If your personal obligations, lifestyle, or relationships do not allow full immersion right now — this is not the right time for you to apply.**

### 2. Tough Skin and Daily Reset
Rejection is part of the job. Every single day. Results in sales have a delayed effect — you plant seeds today that pay off weeks from now. That means you will have hard days where nothing works and you feel like you're going backwards.

What separates the agents who make it: they show up the next day with a clean slate. **No bitterness carried over from yesterday. No frustration from one customer bleeding into the next call.** You have to be able to reset, again and again.

The core belief required: *if somebody else can deliver results in this role, so can I.* You need a deep, burning confidence in yourself — not arrogance, but genuine belief that you are capable.

### 3. Above-Average Social Skills
If social interactions drain you, if talking to strangers is genuinely hard, if being likable doesn't come naturally — this is not the right role. **You must enjoy talking to people.** Our clients are American, they are discerning, and they will buy from someone they like and trust.

You don't need to be the loudest person in the room. But you must be warm, engaging, and naturally easy to talk to. Social ability is not optional here — it is the job.

### 4. Hunger to Earn
You must want to make good money — genuinely, not just as a thing to say. **The "do whatever it takes" attitude has to be real.** Commission-based means your income is directly tied to your output. Agents who treat this like a clock-in/clock-out job earn accordingly. Agents who are hungry and persistent earn well.

If your primary goal is stability and a predictable paycheck, this structure will frustrate you. If your primary goal is to earn as much as your effort allows — this is built for you.

### 5. Willingness and Ability to Learn
People underestimate how much skill matters in sales and overestimate how much luck matters. **Luck is a byproduct of skill and mindset — not the other way around.** The agents who improve fastest are the ones who study their own calls, take feedback seriously, refine their approach, and keep learning.

You will be trained from zero. But you have to meet us halfway. If you resist coaching or think you already know everything, you will not grow here.

### 6. Growth Mindset — No Poor Person Mentality
We are hiring a small number of agents, and every one of them must want to become a top performer. We are not building a mediocre team.

**"Poor person mindset" is disqualifying** — meaning: giving up when it gets hard, making excuses instead of results, accepting the bottom when the top is available. You will fail. Everybody does early on. What matters is the burning desire to get back up and generate results anyway.

We want people who see a setback as information, not a verdict.

### How These Requirements Should Show Up in Content
- **Don't hide the hard parts.** Candidates who are scared off by hearing about 10–12 hour days were never the right fit. Saying it upfront saves everyone's time.
- **Use the requirements as content hooks.** *"Most people can't do this job. Here's why."* is a stronger hook than *"We're hiring!"*
- **The right candidates will lean in, not back off.** Honest, tough-sounding requirements attract ambitious people and repel timewasters. That's the goal.
