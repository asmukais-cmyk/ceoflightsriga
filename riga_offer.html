3. Office Override (Revenue Share)

The override applies only to the portion of GP that exceeds each threshold (graduated model):

GP Range Override Applied To

$70,000 – $99,999 5% Amount above $70,000

Example: If the Riga office generates $120,000 GP in a month:
$0 – $69,999 $0

$100,000 – $120,000 → 7% × $20,000 $1,400
