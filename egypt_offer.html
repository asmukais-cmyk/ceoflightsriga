1. Enhanced Personal Production (Ongoing)

Personal sales commissions are paid at enhanced fixed rates to provide stable income while dedicating time to team building:

  • New & Return Customers: 35% fixed commission.
  • Own Contacts: 50% fixed commission (the previous $10,000 GP threshold is waived).

2. Phase 1: Remote Supervisor & Team Building

During this phase, the primary objective is to recruit, train, and manage a high-performing remote team of agents.

  • Remote Team Override: 8% override on the Gross Profit (GP) generated by all agents you manage, capped at $40,000 GP (max override: $3,200/month).
    The remote supervisor model supports teams producing up to $40,000/month. Beyond that threshold, the office model (Phase 3) is designed to scale your earnings further.
Example: Your team generates $35,000 GP in a month:
8% × $35,000 = $2,800 supervisor income.

If your team generates $55,000 GP:
8% × $40,000 (cap) = $3,200 supervisor income.
* Plus personal sales commissions on your own deals on top.

3. Phase 2: Physical Office Launch

The transition to a physical office in Egypt is strictly performance-based to ensure sustainable growth.

  • Office Opening Trigger: The remote team must generate $40,000+ in Total GP for two (2) consecutive months. Once achieved, you are authorized to scout and lease an office space.
  • Office Launch Bonus: A $2,000 one-time bonus, payable upon the successful setup and official opening of the physical office.
